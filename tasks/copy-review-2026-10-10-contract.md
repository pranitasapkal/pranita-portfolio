## 2. Contract overview
- **H2:** A yes on the phone, a surprise at billing → **A yes over email, a surprise at billing**
- **Body (new):** Contracts were agreed, chased and closed over calls, emails and spreadsheets. A yes had no record, and a wrong rate only surfaced at billing. I designed both sides of Valmo's contract system: an admin panel where the Central Linehaul team turns each month's route plan into contracts and assigns them to transporters, and the Contract Management panel where the transporter answers. Every contract can be accepted, rejected, disputed or ended on screen, with the money shown before each decision, and every answer lands back with the linehaul team as named work.

## 3. Who it's for (second card)
- **Title:** Valmo linehaul team → **Central Linehaul team**
- **Text:** Valmo's team that books trucks for long routes. Each month they assign every route to a transporter at a rate, chase every answer and settle disputes before the start date. This ran on Excel sheets, calls and emails, so I also designed the admin panel they now do it in.
- **Lead-in:** Agreeing by phone broke down in two places → **Agreeing over calls and emails broke down in two places.**
