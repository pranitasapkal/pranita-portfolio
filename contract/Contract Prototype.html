How Contract Management works

Your contracts move through four stages. Here is what to do at each one:

  1. Pending: a new contract from Valmo is waiting. Accept it, or reject it with a reason. If only the rate is wrong, enter your rate and Valmo can resend the contract.
  2. Upcoming: you accepted it and it has not started yet.
  3. Active: trips run every day. Raise a dispute if something is wrong; Valmo replies within 5 days. If Valmo approves part of your rate, review and accept the new rate. If you terminate, the contract stays active for 24hrs, then moves to Closed.
  4. Closed: completed, ended early, cancelled, rejected and no-response contracts stay here for your records.
