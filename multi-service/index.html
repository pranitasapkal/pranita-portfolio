Five services, one app, every age group

What Huntment Ride does

Huntment Ride puts five services behind one account: taxi bookings, limousines for meetings, Umrah packages, food delivery and grocery shopping. I designed the app's flows, wireframes and hi-fi screens.

Easy to navigate at any age

Turn five unrelated services into one app that is easy to find your way around, for people from 18 to past 55.

One outing meant several apps

A taxi, a meal and a grocery run each needed a different app. Planning one outing meant switching between them.

Four problems
01
Scattered services
A separate app for every service, each one taking up storage.
02
Too much coordination
One outing needed planning across several apps.
03
Umrah was hard to arrange
A pilgrimage needs visas, transport and stays, which people usually leave to an expert.
04
Safety worries
Every extra app is one more place for personal and payment data to leak.
Four answers
01
One platform
Taxi, meetings, Umrah, food and groceries in one app, with one account.
02
Plan it in one place
Book different services from the same interface, one after another.
03
Remembered preferences
Tailored recommendations, and preferences remembered for each service.
04
Guided special services
A step-by-step flow for Umrah, with the requirements stated up front.

Booking a ride, a meal or an Umrah trip

A +971 number and a one-time code

A splash screen and three onboarding screens explain the app. Sign-up takes a name, email and +971 number, confirms it with a one-time code, then asks for location.

Every service on one grid

Every service sits on one grid: Taxi, Meetings, Umrah, Food, Groceries and More, under rotating offers. The side menu holds history, complaints, referral, settings and help.

Ride now, or book for later

Pick from and to, then choose a nearby car by seats, distance and arrival time. The driver card shows the fare and payment, with call and message. Payment offers the wallet, cards, pay later and cash.

A meeting stop between from and to

Limousine bookings for meetings add a third field, the meeting location, between from and to, so one booking covers a route with a stop.

Umrah requirements shown before payment

Choose flight, bus or limousine and see what is included: visa, transfers, stay, border taxes and a guide. Compare Normal, 4 star and 5 star packages from AED 4,300, then add passengers and pay. The visa documents and processing time sit on the package page, before payment.

Food tracked in four steps

Browse by category, open a restaurant and add to the cart. Checkout takes notes for the driver and a tip, then the order moves through four tracked steps. Cancelling asks for a reason from a list.

A grocery list that repeats monthly

Groceries work on demand, and also as a monthly list: tick the items you always buy, repeat every month, then pick a date and a delivery time.
