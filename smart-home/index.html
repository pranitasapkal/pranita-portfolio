Control, monitor and automate from one phone

The brief

AuraSmart is a smart home management app. Residents control lighting, security, climate and entertainment from one mobile app, and I designed it from research to hi-fi screens.

Six things the app had to do

  • An easy mobile app for home automation.
  • Work with smart lights, thermostats, security cameras and smart locks.
  • Control and monitor many devices remotely, all at once.
  • Real-time status for every device.
  • Schedules and routines for devices.
  • Insights on energy use and device activity.

Rival apps took five steps to make a change

I compared nine apps feature by feature, then looked closely at three.

Google Nest
  • Pros: simple device setup, visual control, easy scheduling, real-time notifications
  • Cons: little customisation, lag, uneven third-party support
Amazon Alexa
  • Pros: device integration, voice, skills, multi-room music
  • Cons: complex device setup, little personalisation, scattered home control, privacy concerns
Fibaro
  • Pros: visual dashboard, climate and security control, custom icons, live power use
  • Cons: outdated design, poor navigation through rooms and devices
User complaints
  • Making a change takes five steps; the path has to be shorter.
  • Settings should be easy to find without extra effort.
  • Devices cannot be prioritised.
  • Only one camera can be seen at a time.
  • No vacation mode to switch lights on and off while you are away.
  • Users want to be able to reach support.

Wireframes

From first launch to an if-then automation

Three onboarding screens, three benefits

The logo shows on launch, then three onboarding screens cover the benefits: control, automation and saving energy.

Sign up with just an email

Registration needs only an email, with Google, Apple, Facebook and Twitter as shortcuts.

Fewer steps to the devices used most

The most-used devices stay at the top. Lights, cameras and electrical devices sit under the weather, then every device by room. The Smart tab holds one-tap scenes.

Find, connect, confirm

The app looks for devices nearby, connects with a progress ring, and confirms with a way to go home or control the device.

Commands without pressing buttons

People say the command instead of pressing a button, which helps anyone with impaired vision or motor function.

Each device gets its own controls

Each device type gets its own control: warmth and colour for a lamp, live view and recording for a camera, temperature and mode for the AC.

Automations start from a trigger

An automation starts from a trigger: a tap, the weather, location, a time, a device's status. Set the condition, then what happens, for example: if it is above 20°C and dry, turn on the living room AC.
